Skate & Skate

To start off, skateboarding is probably one of the best things to do in life. Even means insane commitment and falls, in the end the adrenaline rush and skateboarding community pays off in the end. As up to now, I have been skating for about 2 summers and there hasn't been a session that wasn't throughly enjoyed. I love skating at night and is hoping to visit some city spot with good obsticles (and sidewalks) with a skate crew. With Canada having insane snow, I am planning to invest in a snowboard to snowboard in the winter. The following will be my gallery of random skateboarding things.


Skate Brands I Used




Yuto Horigome



My favourite professional skateboarder is Yuto Horigome. He is a professional skateboarder from Japan who was born on January 19, 1999, in Tokyo. He is known for his technical skills and consistency in competitions, as well as his smooth and effortless style in executing extremely difficult tricks. He first gained attention in the skateboarding world in 2016 when he won the Tampa Am contest, and he has since become one of the most highly regarded street skateboarders in the world. In 2021, he won the first-ever Olympic gold medal in skateboarding, in the men's street event at the Tokyo Olympics. In fact almost every competition nowadays that he enters he usually wins first.

Jonny Giger



My second favourite professional skateboarder is Jonny Giger. Jonny Giger is a professional skateboarder from Switzerland who was born on May 6, 1997. He is known for his technical abilities and his popular YouTube channel, where he shares videos of himself skateboarding, doing trick tutorials, and documenting his daily life. He is extremely good at flat ground tricks and is known for being able to replicate extremely hard tricks, such as tricks from Rodney Mullen.
